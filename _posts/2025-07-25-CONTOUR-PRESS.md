My [paper on a large-scale comparison of humans and DNNs](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ftR9OuiUJA) has been picked up by media:

[Nik Papageorgiou](https://people.epfl.ch/n.papageorgiou?lang=en) from the EPFL press wrote a fantastic article on my work which can be read for free [here](https://actu.epfl.ch/news/ai-can-t-see-as-well-as-humans-and-how-to-fix-it/).

In addition, EPFL covered the work on its front page! Certainly a proud and humbling note to finish my Ph.D. with, having privately defended my thesis successfully just a couple weeks prior. Thank you EPFL! 

![EPFL Front Page](https://benlonnqvist.github.io/assets/images/EPFL_frontpage.png)

Local Swiss media also picked up the article. 20 Minutes, a Swiss French-speaking magazine [read by over 400,000 people daily](https://www.20min.ch/fr/story/un-grand-merci-vous-etes-403-000-a-lire-20-minutes-chaque-jour-103320129) published an article on the work, which you can read for free [here](https://www.20min.ch/fr/story/epfl-le-cerveau-humain-depasse-l-ia-pour-reconnaitre-les-formes-103386131).
