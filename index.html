About

I'm Head of NeuroAI at Read/Write Labs (formerly Dandelion Science), where we use computational models of the brain to develop targeted therapies for brain disorders. I architect the company's generative neuromodulation platform.

I did my PhD in Computational Neuroscience at EPFL (2020–2025), advised by Michael Herzog and Martin Schrimpf. My thesis, Differences in Visual Perception in Humans and Deep Neural Networks, combined psychophysics, neural data and model-building. I'm also a core contributor to the open-source Brain-Score platform. Before that I studied Economics and Finance at the University of Aberdeen.
