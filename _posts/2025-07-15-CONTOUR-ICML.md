Our [new paper on a large-scale comparison of humans and DNNs](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ftR9OuiUJA) has been presented at ICML 2025! We show that many models fail when object contours are fragmented, and that the ability to do contour integration is critical for performance and robustness.
