Our paper on: [Latent Noise Segmentation](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/lonnqvist24a.html) has been accepted at ICML 2024! We show that neural noise can be used for unsupervised segmentation, and doing so reproduces many hallmark results of human perceptual grouping.
