New paper with Elsa Scialom, Alban Bornet and Michael Herzog in the *Journal of Neural Engineering*. We use deep networks to predict which image features are diagnostic for recognising an object. Rendering those features in simulated prosthetic vision improves how well people recognise objects.

- [Paper (DOI)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/ae9227)
