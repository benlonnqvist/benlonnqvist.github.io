My paper on [Latent Noise Segmentation](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/lonnqvist24a.html), published at the top conference ICML, has recently been picked up by the Finnish press. I gave an interview to Finnish journalist Raili Leino at EPFL.

The article was published in the Finnish magazine Tekniikka&Talous, the largest Finnish magazine on technical subjects. In fact, the magazine reaches over 500,000 people annually.
